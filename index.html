Your music. One place.

NIRUNE is a music application for Windows and Android that brings local music and connected provider catalogs into one calm, source-aware experience.

See what we are building How NIRUNE handles data
In development

A player built around the listener.

The current Windows prototype supports local-library playback and an official Google connection for user-initiated YouTube catalog search. Additional provider capabilities are added only when they can be kept isolated, honest, and replaceable.

01

One search surface

Explore local music and available connected catalogs without treating every source as a separate app.

02

Source-aware playback

NIRUNE shows where a track comes from and only offers playback when an authorized source can resolve it.

03

Private by default

Your library stays on your device. Provider credentials are kept in platform-secure storage, away from the interface.

Trust by design

Your account is not our product.

  • No NIRUNE advertising or analytics.
    The public site contains no tracking scripts.
  • No NIRUNE cloud account today.
    The preview stores its library and connected sessions on the device.
  • Disconnect means disconnect.
    Removing Google from NIRUNE deletes its saved OAuth credential from secure local storage.
