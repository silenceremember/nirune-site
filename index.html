NIRUNE

A private-first music application for Windows and Android. NIRUNE brings local music and connected provider catalogs into one interface.

Current functionality

01

Local music

Add folders, search your local library, and play audio files on Windows.

02

Connected catalogs

Connect Google through OAuth and submit explicit searches to the official YouTube Data API.

03

Source-aware design

Online playback is offered only when an authorized provider source supports it.

Data and privacy

The preview has no NIRUNE cloud account or analytics service. Library data and provider credentials remain on the device. Google credentials are kept in platform-secure storage and deleted from NIRUNE when the provider is disconnected.

Read the Privacy Policy

Availability

NIRUNE is under development and is not available as a public download yet. This website documents the product and its data practices.
